Meraki Studios

meraki

The word “Meraki” means doing something with love and care – and that’s the spirit of this place.

The Studios

In the quiet of the valley six intimate Studios await, each with a great vista of the ever-changing beauty of Crete’s nature. Here, simplicity equals luxury.

The Garden

The large 6500 m² garden surrounding the building is a green paradise, filled with old olive, oak, fig, nut and all kinds of fruit trees.

Nature

Among the locals the plot goes by the name of Tsikalaria. It used to be cultivated by a man who had a reputation to be particularly knowledgeable of plants. We want to honour this heritage.

We believe in the power of simplicity, living in harmony with nature and focusing on essentials.

Each studio has a large partly shaded patio and the transition between indoor and outdoor living is seamless. The building is organically integrated in the surrounding nature and invites you to appreciate the moment – enjoy tranquility and connect with the land.

KissosRethymnoCrete

Nestled in the beautiful landscape just a short stroll away from the charming village of Kissos, this spot is a quiet escape into traditional Crete. Surrounded by a tapestry of traditionally cultivated land with olive groves, small vineyards, vegetable gardens and wild nature it’s a world away from mass tourism.

Explore some of the most beautiful beaches on the rural south coast like Ligres and Triopetra about a 25 minute drive away, or dive into Rethymno’s vibrant dining and culture in half an hour. You can wander nearby villages or hike the stunning Psiloritis National Park.

Architecture

Sigurd Larsen

At Meraki Studios, Sigurd Larsen’s vision is brought to life through the raw elegance of concrete, an exposed skeleton that stands proudly as a three-dimensional grid. This structure, shaped by the land, mirrors the rugged form of an abstract rock, it’s cool stone walls crafted from the earth itself, offering a refuge from the Mediterranean sun.

“For years, we talked about leaving the hustle of city life behind. In 2022, we made the move to Crete with our children, and our vision was simple: to create a space that allows you to reconnect with mother nature, that nurtures us, our family, and our guests. Crete, with its natural beauty, offered the perfect place to bring our dream to life.”

Jan & Vanessa

FAQ

Terms and Conditions

When can I check in and check out?

The rent will become effective on the first day of the agreed rental period at 15.00 hours local time and ends on the last day of the agreed rental period at 12.00 hours local time. Special agreements to the check-in and check- out time can be agreed in advance with the lessor due to availability.

Is there a minimum stay?

3 nights

What is included in the price?

The rent includes the expenses for electricity and water use, all taxes final cleaning and wifi.

How do I pay?

Within three (3) working days after the booking the lessee must have transferred 50% of the total amount to the bank account of the lessor. This will serve to confirm the booking, making it peremptory.

What if I need to cancel?

The deposit is refundable until 30 days before arrival. At 30 days before arrival, the balance is due and the total payment is non-refundable. sole exception: In case we can rent out the apartment/studio within this time of 30 days we refund the sum paid minus 200 Euros cancellation fee.

Is there a washing machine?

We have a washing machine so you can stay fresh and clean even if you travel light (5 euros per load)

Can we rent the whole house?

The building with all six units is also perfect to be rented entirely by a group.

How do we get there?

The house is accessible with car or scooter, taxi or public transport.

Are pets allowed? Can I smoke?

Pets are not allowed. Inside the apartment(s) smoking is not allowed.

Can we barbecue?

Open fires are not allowed by law. No BBQ/ grill charcoal or grill coal can be used.

Can children use the pool?

Children should not use the swimming pool unattended
